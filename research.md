## SLIDE 3: KERRISDALE CAPITAL — "DAT AIN'T NO STRATEGY"

Published: October 2025. Kerrisdale Capital was short BMNR.

Key findings from Kerrisdale's report:

1. **The flywheel is broken**: Strategy (MSTR) sustained a 2–2.5x mNAV premium for years. BMNR's premium collapsed from 2x to 1.2x within months of launch. Without a premium, issuing stock to buy ETH is just dilution.

2. **$10B+ in stock issued in 3 months**: "roughly $170 million per day" of new stock issued. "The sheer velocity of BMNR's stock issuance has turned early enthusiasm into fatigue."

3. **Obfuscation of shares outstanding**: BMNR "quietly dropped up-to-date share counts from its weekly press releases," making mNAV calculation impossible for retail investors.

4. **The $365M offering was not accretive**: After accounting for attached warrants (valued at ~$913M potential proceeds at $87.50 strike), the effective deal price was well below the stated $70/share. Kerrisdale called it "a cleverly packaged dilutive raise that sacrificed long-term credibility for short-term cash."

5. **Better alternatives exist**: ETH ETFs, spot ETH, or direct staking — all give exposure without the dilution, management fees, or mNAV premium risk.

- Source: Kerrisdale Capital, "DAT Ain't No Strategy," October 8, 2025
- URL: https://www.kerrisdalecap.com/investments/bitmine-immersion-bmnr/

## SLIDE 5: THE STRUCTURAL TRAP — WHY BMNR CAN'T RECOVER

### The Broken Flywheel

The DAT model requires:
1. Stock trades at premium to NAV → issuing stock at premium buys more token per share than dilution costs
2. Token price rises → NAV increases → premium sustained
3. More capital raised → more tokens → virtuous cycle

**When ANY condition fails, the cycle reverses:**
- Stock falls below NAV → issuing stock destroys shareholder value
- ETH falls → unrealized losses → institutional investors exit
- Premium collapses → raises become dilutive → retail capitulates

BMNR's premium has already collapsed. ETH is at $1,555. The company is still buying.

### The Staking Revenue Mirage

BitMine claims $296M annualized staking revenue at 2.73% yield on 4.7M staked ETH.

**Reality check**:
- 2.73% on ETH worth $1,555 = ~$200M annualized (not $296M — that used $2,003 ETH)
- Total capital deployed: ~$17.6B
- Staking yield covers: **~1.1% of invested capital per year**
- The ETH has lost ~$9B in value
- To recover the loss via staking alone at $1,555 ETH: **~45 years**

### The 10-Year Lock-In

BitMine signed a **10-year consulting agreement with Ethereum Tower LLC** to implement the ETH treasury strategy. Early termination triggers **liquidated damages**.

The company is contractually obligated to continue this strategy regardless of ETH's price.

- Source: SEC 8-K, July 2025 (StockTitan summary)

### Share Count: The Dilution Doom Loop

- Pre-pivot (FY2024): ~2 million shares
- Q1 2026: 455 million shares — **+22,650% in 18 months**
- Simply Wall St. flags: "shareholders have been substantially diluted in the past year (over 286x increase in shares outstanding)"
- Change since IPO: **-99.90%** (adjusted for splits)
